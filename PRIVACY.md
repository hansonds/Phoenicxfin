# Phoenicxfin Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 20, 2026*

Phoenicxfin is a free, open-source app for watching media from a **Jellyfin server that you or someone you trust runs**. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What Phoenicxfin collects
**Nothing is collected by the developer.** Phoenicxfin has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting, and it never sends your data to the developer or to any third party.

## What is stored on your device
To work, the app saves the following only on your TV or device:
- The address of the Jellyfin server you connect to
- Your Jellyfin login session (an access token, not your password)
- Your app settings, such as player and subtitle preferences

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

## What is sent to your Jellyfin server
The app talks **only to the Jellyfin server you enter**. Like every Jellyfin app, it sends that server:
- Your username and password when you sign in
- What you watch and your playback progress, so the server can resume playback and mark items as watched
- Basic device information (device name, app name and version) so the server can list the device in its dashboard

That server is not operated by the Phoenicxfin developer. How it handles data is decided by whoever runs it.

## Children
Phoenicxfin is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
